Ask a question
Is this still the PocketBase I know?

Yes. Your instance runs PocketBase with its familiar admin dashboard, collections, authentication, file storage, and APIs. PBManager takes care of the hosting around it.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Add your domain, follow the DNS and ownership verification steps, and track HTTPS setup from the manager. Your instance keeps its default address as well.

Can I try the dashboard first?

Absolutely. Open the interactive demo and try creating, stopping, or restarting a sample instance. It runs locally in your browser and doesn’t touch a real server.

How do I get hosting, and what does it cost?

Request access and tell us about your project. Availability, resources, and pricing will be confirmed before you commit. This page doesn’t take payments.
